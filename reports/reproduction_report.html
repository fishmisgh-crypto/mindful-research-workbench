MINDFUL, reproduced

An independent Python reproduction of the MINDFUL neural-instability measure (Pun et al., Communications Biology 7, 1363, 2024) from the released recordings and code. MINDFUL compares each minute of intracortical activity with a reference day using the Kullback–Leibler divergence (KLD) and asks how closely that drift tracks the cursor's angular error.

Summary

With the released code's settings (upstream preset), 4 of 4 primary results are within ±0.02 of the published Pearson r and Spearman ρ, and the angular-error-binned result (Fig. 1b) reproduces to three decimals.

For participant T11, keeping the outlier trials that the released script removes puts all four feature sets within 0.005 of the published values (largest difference 0.072 without them). This suggests the published T11 figures included those trials. It is evidence, not proof; the presets are left as specified and the change is reported below as a single-parameter run.

A correlation describes how closely KLD tracks decoder error; it is not an accuracy. KLD is a divergence in nats, not a percentage.
